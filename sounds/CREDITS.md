# Sounds

White / pink / brown are generated offline (seamless PCM loops), then high-passed
so phone speakers can actually play them.

Fan, Soft, and AC are real CC0 recordings from Freesound, looped and level-matched:

- **Fan** — [Floor Fan (loop)](https://freesound.org/s/831775/) by NickTayloe
- **Soft** — [Space Heater, Seamless Loop](https://freesound.org/s/753544/) by HECKFRICKER
- **AC** — [AC fan loop](https://freesound.org/s/189988/) by raygrote
